# Assignment 1 – Idea Pitch: *Prodajen Uje*

## Project Title  
**Prodajen Uje**

## Project Overview  
*Prodajen Uje* is a web platform that connects small olive oil producers with customers who value authentic, locally made olive oil.  
It helps local producers promote and sell their products online while giving customers an easy way to discover, compare, and buy high-quality oils directly from the source.

## Problem Statement  
Small olive oil producers often struggle to reach a wider audience and compete with large commercial brands.  
Meanwhile, customers who appreciate authentic, local olive oil find it difficult to discover and purchase directly from trusted small-scale producers.

## Target Users  
- Small olive oil producers who don't have a platform or customers to sell their oil to.  
- Customers who appreciate authentic, high-quality, locally produced olive oil.

## Proposed Solution  
*Prodajen Uje* provides a digital marketplace where small producers can register, list, and sell their olive oil.  
Buyers can browse, search, and filter by location, olive variety, harvest year, and more, ensuring a transparent and easy way to connect with genuine local producers.

## How Did We Think of the Idea?  
Both team members have family-owned olive trees and produce olive oil, so this is something close to us that came up naturally during brainstorming for platform ideas.
